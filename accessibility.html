Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated: August 2026

We want this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people using a keyboard only, a screen reader, magnification, or a setting that reduces motion.

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. We have not had an independent accessibility audit, and we are not certified or accredited against any accessibility standard. This statement describes what we have built and tested ourselves, and what we know is not finished.

What we have built in

  • A "skip to main content" link as the first item on every page.
  • Keyboard access to all navigation, links, form fields and buttons, with a visible focus outline that also works on dark sections.
  • Headings in a logical order, and page regions marked up so a screen reader can navigate by landmark.
  • Form fields with visible, programmatically associated labels, and validation messages in text rather than colour alone.
  • Decorative graphics hidden from assistive technology, and meaningful icons and controls given accessible names.
  • Touch and click targets in the navigation and footer sized to at least 44 by 44 pixels.
  • Respect for the operating system "reduce motion" setting: scroll and entrance animation is reduced or removed when that setting is on.
  • A responsive layout that works down to 375 pixels wide, and text that reflows when you zoom.
  • Pages marked as British English so screen readers pronounce them correctly.

Known limitations

We would rather list these than imply they do not exist.

  • No independent audit. Our testing is internal — keyboard, screen reader spot checks, zoom and reduced motion. It is not a substitute for a formal audit or for testing with disabled users.
  • The jobs list needs JavaScript. Live roles are loaded dynamically. With JavaScript unavailable, the jobs pages show a fallback message rather than the list. You can always reach us directly for the current roles.
  • The home page is motion-heavy. It uses scroll-linked animation. This is reduced when "reduce motion" is on, but the page is still visually busier than the rest of the site.
  • Third-party content. Our booking link opens Google Calendar, which we do not control. Role descriptions and documents originate from employers, and we cannot guarantee their formatting is accessible.
  • Colour contrast. We have checked contrast on text and controls, but some large display type and decorative detail sits closer to the minimum than we would like.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site stops you doing what you came to do, please tell us — it is the fastest way for us to fix it.

  • Email hello@surfacetalent.co.uk with the page and what happened.
  • Or use any route on our contact page.

We will acknowledge your message and tell you what we can do and when. If you cannot use a form or upload on this site, we will take the information another way — including by phone or email — so that an inaccessible page never costs you a role.

Applying for a role another way

If the application form does not work for you, email your CV and the role you are interested in to hello@surfacetalent.co.uk. We will treat it exactly as we would a form submission. If you need a reasonable adjustment at any stage — including at interview — tell us and we will arrange it with the employer.

How this statement is maintained

We review this page when we change the site materially, and at least once a year. If we commission an independent audit, or achieve a formal conformance level, we will say so here and date it.
