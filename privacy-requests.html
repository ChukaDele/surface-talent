Privacy requests

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains how to see, change or remove the information we hold about you, how to object to what we are doing with it, and how to complain. It applies to candidates, clients and website visitors.

One route for everything: email privacy@surfacetalent.co.uk and tell us what you want. If that address does not reach us for any reason, use hello@surfacetalent.co.uk and mark it "privacy request". Requests are free and you do not need to use any particular form of words.

What you can ask for

  • Access — a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and where we got it.
  • Correction — fix anything inaccurate, out of date or incomplete. For candidates this includes experience your CV understated.
  • Deletion — remove your information where we have no overriding reason to keep it.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests, and object to direct marketing at any time. Marketing objections take effect without exception.
  • Restriction — pause what we do with your information while a dispute about it is resolved.
  • Portability — receive information you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format, where that right applies.
  • Withdraw consent — where consent is what we relied on, such as job alerts.
  • Human review of an assessment — ask a person to look again at an assessment that affects you, and tell us why you think it is wrong. See fair recruitment and responsible AI.
  • Complain — about how we have handled your information.

What to include

You do not have to explain why you are asking. To help us find the right record and avoid disclosing your information to the wrong person, it helps if you tell us:

  1. your name, and the email address or phone number you dealt with us on;
  2. what you want — for example "send me everything you hold", "delete my record", or "correct my job title";
  3. for a correction, what is wrong and what it should say;
  4. for human review, which role or assessment you mean and what you think was missed;
  5. a rough date range, if you only want part of the record.

If we cannot confirm who you are from the request, we will ask for enough proof of identity to be satisfied — and no more than that. We will not ask you to send identity documents by insecure means.

How long we take

  • We acknowledge requests as soon as we can.
  • We respond within one calendar month of receiving your request, and of receiving any identity confirmation we needed.
  • If a request is complex, or you have made several, we may extend by up to two further months. We will tell you within the first month if that happens, and why.
  • Marketing objections and unsubscribes are actioned promptly and do not wait on the above.

When we may not be able to do everything you ask

Some rights have limits in law. For example we may need to keep certain records to meet a legal duty, to establish or defend a legal claim, or where information about you is bound up with information about someone else. If we cannot fully meet a request, we will tell you which part, and why.

If you are not happy with our response

Tell us — reply to us directly and we will look at it again.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or by calling their helpline. You do not have to come to us first, and complaining to the ICO is free.

If your concern is about our conduct as a recruitment business rather than about your information — for example being asked for a fee to find work — you can report it to the Fair Work Agency, which enforces recruitment sector rules in Great Britain.
