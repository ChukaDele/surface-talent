Cookie policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work properly and provide information to the site owner about how visitors use it.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for two purposes: to make the website function correctly, and to understand how visitors use it so we can improve the experience. We do not use cookies for advertising or behavioural targeting.

4. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. The links below explain how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Blocking analytics cookies will not affect the functionality of the website.

We may update this cookie policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly.
