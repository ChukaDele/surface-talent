Cookie policy

Last updated: August 2026

This website sets no cookies. There is no analytics, no session recording, no advertising or social media tracking, and no cross-site profiling. That is why you are not asked to accept or reject cookies when you arrive.

1. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small file a website stores on your device. Related technologies — local storage and session storage — do the same job in a different way. UK rules on cookies also cover those, so this page describes everything this site stores on your device, not just cookies.

2. Cookies we set

None. We do not set first-party cookies, and we do not load anything that sets third-party cookies on our pages.

If you are used to seeing a cookie banner on every site, its absence here is deliberate: a banner is required when a site wants to store non-essential information on your device. We do not, so there is nothing to ask you about. If that ever changes, we will ask for your consent before setting anything non-essential — and this page will be updated first.

3. What we do store on your device

Two items, only inside the tab you are using, and only once you reach a page that carries an enquiry or application form.

NameTypePurposeLasts
st_landing Session storage Remembers the page you were on when the form script first loaded, so that if you send us an enquiry we can see which page it came from and reply in context. Until you close the tab
st_utm Session storage Remembers campaign parameters (such as utm_source) if you arrived from a link that carried them, so an enquiry can be attributed to the right campaign. Until you close the tab

These are first-party only. They are not cookies, they are not sent to any third party, they are not used to build a profile of you, and they are deleted when you close the tab. If you never submit a form, they are never read for any purpose. To avoid them entirely, use a private or incognito window, or block storage for this site in your browser settings — the site works normally either way.

4. Third parties your browser contacts

Some parts of the site are served from other companies' networks. Those companies necessarily see your IP address and basic request information, because that is how the web works. None of them set cookies on our pages, and none of them are analytics or advertising services.

ServiceWhat it is forSets cookies?
Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com)Serves the typefaces used across the siteNo
jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net)Serves the open-source animation and smooth-scrolling libraries used on the home pageNo
CloudflareHosts this website and serves every pageNo

Two things people often assume are here, and are not:

  • Our jobs list does not contact Airtable from your browser. Role data is held in Airtable, but it is fetched by our own server and passed to the page from our domain. Your browser never talks to Airtable, and Airtable sets nothing on your device.
  • Our forms do not use a third-party form service. They post to our own endpoint on this domain, which forwards the submission to our internal systems. No external form provider is loaded into the page.

If you click through to a booking link, that opens Google Calendar on Google's own site, where Google's terms and cookies apply. The same is true of any other external site we link to.

5. What we deliberately do not do

  • No Google Analytics, and no other analytics product.
  • No session recording, heatmaps or screen replay.
  • No advertising, remarketing or conversion pixels.
  • No social media tracking pixels or embedded trackers.
  • No fingerprinting, and no attempt to identify you across other websites.

We may add basic, privacy-respecting analytics in future. If we do, it will appear in this policy first, and anything non-essential will be off until you agree to it.

6. Managing storage in your browser

You can block or clear cookies and site storage at any time. Doing so will not break this website.

How we handle personal information is set out in our privacy policy and, for candidates, our candidate privacy notice. To exercise a data protection right, see privacy requests.

8. Changes and contact

If we change what this site stores, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Questions: privacy@surfacetalent.co.uk.
