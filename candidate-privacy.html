Candidate privacy notice

Last updated: August 2026

This notice is for candidates and anyone considering working with us. It explains what information we hold about you, where it comes from, what we do with it, and how you can question or change it. It is written to be read without legal training.

If you are a client, supplier or general website visitor, our general privacy policy applies to you instead.

The short version. We keep a record of your professional experience so we can put you forward for surface engineering roles. We use our own software, including AI-assisted tools, to compare that experience against what a role actually needs. A recruiter, not the software, decides who to put forward — and we will not send your CV to an employer without asking you first.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Surface Talent is the data controller for the candidate information described in this notice. That means we decide how it is used and we are accountable for it.

For anything to do with your information, contact privacy@surfacetalent.co.uk. If that address does not reach us for any reason, use hello@surfacetalent.co.uk.

2. What information we hold about you

  • Who you are and how to reach you: name, email address, telephone number, and the general area you live or want to work in.
  • Your professional background: your CV, employment history, current and previous employers, job titles, qualifications, tickets and certifications, technical skills, and the processes and plant you have worked with.
  • What you are looking for: the kind of role you want, disciplines and functions of interest, employment type, notice period, and current or desired salary or rate.
  • Your professional profile: a link to your LinkedIn or equivalent professional profile, where you have given it to us or where it forms part of the record we received.
  • Right to work: confirmation of your right to work in the UK, and supporting documents where a placement requires them.
  • Our conversations: notes from calls and meetings, emails and messages, and — where an interview has taken place and you have been told about it — an interview transcript or recording.
  • Assessment records: the requirements framework used for a role, the evidence we identified for and against each requirement, the resulting fit indicators, and how complete the evidence was.
  • References and feedback: references you have asked us to take up, and feedback from an employer about your application or interview.

Information we do not ask for

We do not ask for special category information — such as health, disability, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, trade union membership or political opinion — or criminal record information, unless there is a specific lawful reason and we tell you why at the time. Two common exceptions are a reasonable adjustment you ask us to arrange for an interview, and a background check a specific employer is legally required to run.

Please do not include this kind of information in your CV or messages unless it is genuinely relevant. If you send it to us anyway, we will remove it or restrict who can see it where we reasonably can.

3. Where your information comes from

  • Directly from you: forms on this website, your CV, emails, calls, messages and interviews.
  • Recruitly: the recruitment platform we use to hold candidate records and to read CVs. Candidate records reach us through it, including records created when you or a previous contact supplied a CV.
  • Professional and public sources: professional networking profiles, employer websites, industry directories, sector publications and event listings — used to identify people with relevant surface engineering experience and to keep a record accurate.
  • Referrals: someone in the industry suggesting we speak to you. If we approach you this way, we will tell you where we got your details if you ask.
  • Employers: feedback on an application or interview, and confirmation of an offer or placement.

If we found you rather than the other way round, we will tell you what we hold the first time we make meaningful contact, and you can ask us to delete it.

5. How we assess candidates, and how AI is involved

Surface engineering roles are often described badly. A job advert can miss the process, the plant, the shift pattern and the actual technical depth a role needs. Our whole approach is to fix that, and our software exists to do it consistently.

In practice this means:

  • We turn what a role genuinely needs into a written set of weighted requirements, built from conversations with the employer rather than from the advert alone.
  • We use software, including AI-assisted tools, to read the evidence we hold about you — your CV, your record, and interview notes or transcripts where they exist — and to identify what supports or does not support each requirement.
  • The result is a set of fit indicators shown alongside the evidence behind them, how much of the requirement set the evidence actually covers, and how confident that assessment is.
  • Where there is no evidence about a requirement, that is recorded as missing evidence — not as a bad result. Gaps like these are usually what we ask you about next.
  • An assessment is pinned to the version of the requirements used at the time, so it is always clear what you were assessed against.
  • When new information arrives — an interview, a correction from you, a detail your CV left out — the assessment can be produced again against the same requirements.

No decision about you is made by software alone. These tools help a recruiter read evidence consistently and see what is missing. A person decides whether to contact you, whether to put you forward, and what to tell an employer. You can ask a human to review any assessment that affects you, and you can ask us to correct the information it was based on.

We do not use protected characteristics — such as age, sex, race, disability, religion or belief — as assessment criteria, and we do not intend them to influence an assessment. We cannot promise that any assessment process is free of bias, and we do not claim that. What we can describe is what the process is built to do, what it shows a recruiter, and how you can challenge it. Our fair recruitment and responsible AI page sets out those safeguards in full.

6. Who we share your information with

  • Employers: we share your details with a prospective employer so you can be considered for a role. We ask you before each submission — we do not send your CV to an employer without your knowledge and permission, and we do not send it out speculatively to build a client list.
  • Your current employer: never, as part of representing you. We will not approach them.
  • Service providers: organisations that process information for us under contract and on our instructions. These are our recruitment platform and CV reading service, our own application and its database, our website and form handling, our email and document storage, and the AI model provider whose service performs the assessment processing described in section 5. They may only use the information to provide their service to us.
  • The EMC Surface Technologies group: we are a sister business of the group. Where the group is the hiring employer, sharing your details is the same as sharing with any other employer — we ask you first. We do not pass candidate records into the group for its own unrelated purposes.
  • Professional advisers, and legal or regulatory bodies: where we are required to share information by law, or need to in order to establish or defend a legal claim.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to train publicly available AI models.

We keep a full internal register of the providers we use. If you want to know exactly who processes your information and where, ask us and we will tell you.

7. Where your information is processed

We are a UK business and your information is handled in the UK wherever we can. Some of our providers process information outside the UK, including in the United States and the European Economic Area.

Where information leaves the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations where they cover the destination, or on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses, together with any additional protections the transfer needs.

8. How long we keep it

  • Candidate records: up to 2 years from our last meaningful contact with you, so we can tell you about roles that suit you. "Meaningful contact" means a real exchange — a conversation, an application, or you replying to us — not an unopened marketing email.
  • Longer than 2 years where there is a continuing reason: an active application or placement, an ongoing working relationship with you, an unresolved complaint or dispute, or a specific legal duty to keep records.
  • Placement and payroll-related records: for the period required by tax, employment and accounting law after the relationship ends.
  • At the end of the applicable period we delete the record or remove the details that identify you.

You can ask us to delete your record sooner — see section 9 — and we will unless we have a legal reason to keep part of it, which we will explain.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • Show you what we hold about you, and where we got it.
  • Correct anything wrong, out of date or incomplete.
  • Delete your information, where we have no overriding reason to keep it.
  • Stop or limit what we do with it, including objecting to our use of legitimate interests, and objecting to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdraw consent where consent is what we relied on, such as job alerts.
  • Receive a copy in a portable, machine-readable format where that right applies.
  • Have a person review an assessment, and tell us why you think it is wrong.

Email privacy@surfacetalent.co.uk, or see how to make a privacy request for what to include. These rights are free to use. We reply within one calendar month, and will tell you if a complex request needs longer.

10. Asking us to look again

If you think an assessment is wrong, or that we have the wrong information about your experience, tell us. This is not a formal process and you do not need to invoke a legal right to use it.

  • Tell us what is wrong or missing — a process you have run, plant you have worked on, a ticket you hold, responsibility your job title hides.
  • We will correct the underlying record.
  • Where an assessment was based on that information, it can be produced again against the same role requirements.
  • A recruiter will look at the result and come back to you.

We cannot promise an employer will change its decision — that is theirs to make, not ours. We can make sure the information we hold and what we said about you is accurate.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, tell us first at privacy@surfacetalent.co.uk and we will try to put it right.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. You do not have to come to us first.

If your concern is about how we behaved as a recruitment business rather than about your information — for example being charged a fee to find work, or pressure to accept a role — you can report it to the Fair Work Agency, which enforces recruitment sector rules in Great Britain. See our modern slavery and ethical recruitment policy.

12. Changes to this notice

If we change how we use candidate information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Where a change materially affects you, we will tell you directly where we reasonably can.
