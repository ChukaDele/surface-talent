Origin

This started in an operating group — not a recruitment agency.

Surface Talent is a sister business of the EMC Surface Technologies group (opens in a new tab). EMC invests in and develops surface-engineering businesses that own and run finishing plants — anodising, electroplating and related processes among them.

We are an external specialist recruitment company — not EMC’s internal talent team. The proximity is the point: briefs, screening and shortlists are shaped by how those plants actually work.

If you’re hiring

Difficult roles. Discreet process.

  • Retained search when the hire warrants it — boards, owners and shareholders included.
  • Off-market approaches by default for senior work; no noisy public ads unless agreed.
  • Shortlists we will defend; involvement through offer and early tenure.

Leadership roles we search

Director-level coverage — stated as coverage.

This is the span of senior roles we search across UK surface engineering. It is not a published list of completed mandates. Anonymised outcomes will be added when approved for public use.

  • Managing Director — P&L leadership of a site or business unit
  • Operations Director — multi-site operations, production, improvement
  • Technical Director — process, chemistry, standards, NPD
  • Commercial / Sales Director — pipeline, pricing, key accounts
  • Quality Director — NADCAP, AS9100, ISO, audit leadership
  • GM / Business Unit Lead — site, division or specialist business

For confidential retained search: thorough brief, discreet market mapping, direct approaches, a shortlist of typically two to three people, and structured check-ins through offer, onboarding and the first 90 days.
